Sensory Manifesto // Accessibility

Integrated Cognitive Design

The interface should adapt to the mind, not the other way around. Unifying high-craft aesthetics with active sensory modulation proves that ambitious visual design and cognitive accessibility are not enemies.

  • Dual-layer architecture: strict separation of decoration (chaos) and action (functional).
  • AttentionGuard presets: instant saturation and motion modulation for ADHD/Autism.
  • Physical attention anchors: reduced gaze drift through calm micro-interactions.
